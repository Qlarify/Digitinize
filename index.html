How we work

From a WhatsApp message to a published video.

Your team should never have to chase a video. Every shoot and every deliverable is tracked from request to delivery, so we can tell you where each one is.

  1. Brief

    Send the request on WhatsApp, mail or a call: doctor, topic, date. We confirm the list of videos from that shoot before anything starts.

  2. Shoot

    A crew arrives on time with camera, audio and lights, and works around OPD hours and the doctor's schedule.

  3. Edit and review

    First cut in [X] working days. One link per shoot; comment on the exact second, and every version is downloadable.

  4. Deliver

    Subtitled, with thumbnails and intro cards, in every size you post. Invoiced against the rate card you approved.

Nothing goes out without your yes. No video is published or shared before you approve it.

Your brand, kept. Hospital names, logos and designations follow each unit's rules.

Medical terms checked. Doctor names and clinical words are verified in every subtitle.

Clean invoices. Every line on the invoice maps to a delivered video.

FAQ

Questions hospital teams ask us.

Anything else? Ask us on the call.

Do you only work with hospitals?

We work only in healthcare: hospital chains, single-site and specialty hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres, and medical-device and life-sciences companies.

Where do you shoot?

Bengaluru and Mysuru every week. Other cities: travel terms to confirm.

How fast is the first cut?

[X] working days from the shoot for a doctor video; [Y] for a patient story. Urgent edits from footage recorded on a phone can be turned around the same day.

Which languages can you subtitle in?

English plus 10+ Indian languages, including Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi and Bengali. Doctor names, designations and medical terms are checked line by line.

How do reviews work?

You get one review link per shoot. Comment on the exact second of any video; every version stays downloadable. Nothing is published or sent to anyone without your approval.

How is patient consent handled?

Our consent practice: signed consent form per patient story, doctor sign-off on medical claims, retention period for raw footage — to confirm.

What does it cost?

Each deliverable has a fixed price on a rate card we agree with you up front, so every invoice matches what was ordered. Units with steady volume move to a monthly content plan with one invoice a month.
