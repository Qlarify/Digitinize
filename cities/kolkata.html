Bengali is not a translation exercise

Kolkata's patients search, share and judge in Bengali. We subtitle and version hospital video in Bengali with native-speaker checks on every line — the language as the city writes it, not as a translation service renders it.

The city's stories are its strength

Kolkata tells stories. A consented patient journey, a doctor who explains with warmth, a hospital's decades in the neighbourhood — these are films this city watches to the end. Our edit pace is built for stories, not stunts.

Planned days from Bengaluru

One or two packed shoot days a month, flights at actual cost, first cuts two working days later. The review and revision flow runs remotely with the same standards we keep at home.

Questions Kolkata teams ask

Do you interview patients in Bengali?

Yes — with a Bengali-speaking interviewer on the shoot day, and native-speaker checks on the subtitles. The patient tells the story in their own words; the film keeps them.

How does the travel model work for Kolkata?

Flights and stay at actual cost, no markup, agreed before the day is locked. Most Kolkata hospitals pick one or two clustered days a month.

Our hospital serves patients from across eastern India, not only Kolkata.

We version for the full catchment — Bengali for the city and the state, and Hindi or other regional languages for the wider eastern mix, on your international and referral desks' advice.

Can we start small?

We recommend it — one department, one monthly rhythm, results measured before the rest of the hospital joins.
