Questions Mangaluru teams ask

How often must you travel to Mangaluru?

One planned day a month is the usual rhythm; some hospitals prefer two days a quarter with a bigger shot list. The plan is yours — we build the calendar around your OPD schedule.

Our consultants also teach at a medical college. Will they have time?

Teaching consultants are often the best on camera and the hardest to book. We bring a topic sheet cut from their own patients' questions and need thirty focused minutes per doctor.

Can patient stories work here? Families can be private.

Yes — Mangaluru families often prefer it, and we build for it: voice-only options, no-face framing, first names only. A private-told story with dignity outperforms a public one without it.

Do you subtitle in Tulu and Konkani for real?

With native-speaker checks on every line, the same standard we hold Kannada to. A medical subtitle that reads wrong in Tulu is worse than no subtitle at all.
