A city where patients choose in four languages

Bengaluru's patients search in Kannada, Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam — often all within one hospital's waiting area. We subtitle and version hospital video in all of them, checked by native speakers, because a reel that reaches only the English searcher reaches barely half the city.

The pace the city's hospitals actually run

Bengaluru's hospital marketing teams publish weekly, sometimes daily. Our production system was built for that rhythm: every request logged, every video tracked, first cuts in two working days, review in one link where comments land on the exact second.

Every kind of hospital, one production partner

From a thirteen-unit hospital group to a single-specialty fertility centre, the same team, the same standards, the same delivery system. We work around OPD hours, medical-director reviews and brand-team sign-offs — because that is what a Bengaluru hospital's approval chain looks like.

Questions Bengaluru teams ask

How fast can a Bengaluru shoot start?

A topic sheet and quote arrive in one working day; the first shoot day is usually bookable within the same week. Regular monthly slots are scheduled around each consultant's OPD hours, a month ahead.

Do you work with hospitals outside the big chains?

Constantly — single-site hospitals, clinics and diagnostic centres across Bengaluru get the same crew discipline and delivery system as a multi-unit group.

Can you cover Mysuru units too?

Yes — we have served Manipal Hospitals' Mysuru units alongside Bengaluru since 2017, on the same schedules and standards.

What about same-week urgent items?

Phone-shot urgent edits and health-day items get priority in the edit queue. Tell us the date and we hold the slot.
