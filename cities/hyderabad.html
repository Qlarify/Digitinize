Questions Hyderabad teams ask

How does a Hyderabad engagement actually run?

We agree on a monthly or quarterly shoot rhythm, your doctors' offices get the calendar a month ahead, and a crew flies in for the shoot day. Everything else — review, edits, subtitles, delivery — runs remotely with two-day first cuts.

We are a corporate group with a central brand team. Can you work through our review chain?

That is our default way of working: every video versioned, every review in one link, every approval on record. Our multi-unit clients run exactly this flow.

Our target patients include NRI families. Do you make for that audience?

Yes — international-desk videos, doctor introductions and arrival walk-throughs, subtitled and versioned for the families abroad who shortlist Hyderabad hospitals from another time zone.

Can you start with one hospital, not the whole group?

We recommend it: one flagship hospital, one monthly rhythm, results measured — then the group's other units follow the calendar that already works.
