The six films this department runs on

Each earns its place. Cut to size, never inflate — a department publishing four of these steadily beats one publishing all six badly.

  • The first consultation, explained — Fifteen minutes couples fear more than the treatment. A fertility specialist describing exactly what happens removes the fear that delays booking.
  • Fertility myths, answered once and for all — "IVF babies are weaker", "rest for three months", "it always fails the first time" — a specialist calmly clearing the field.
  • What actually affects your chances — Age, AMH, weight, smoking, timing — honest numbers without false hope. Honesty is the differentiator in this specialty, and video is where it shows.
  • Male-factor fertility film — Half the story, almost never spoken. A urologist-and-andrologist segment normalises the conversation couples avoid.
  • Egg freezing: the honest explainer — For the career-aged audience that searches quietly. Who it works for, what it costs in time, what it cannot promise.
  • Embryology lab tour — Micro-injection b-roll, the grading screens, the cryo tanks — filmed to the lab director's rules, it becomes every centre's most-watched asset.

Consent and dignity in IVF & Fertility video

Fertility stories are the most private in medicine. Consent means: identity controls (first names, voice-only options), filmed with the couple's own counsellor present, and the cut shown before publication. Success numbers appear only from your centre's own audited outcomes, framed per regulatory guidance — no guarantees, no comparisons. Aggregator platforms sell price; you sell trust and process, and only your own video can carry the embryologist saying "this is how we handle your embryos."

Start with the paperwork: our patient video consent kit is free and printable — signed before any recording, filed with the project record.

Measuring it honestly

Fertility attribution is unusually clean: couples remember where their trust formed. Ask every first consultation "what did you watch?" and log it; track the lab tour's completion rate (it is the decision film); and measure the myth video's shares against enquiry-call origins.
