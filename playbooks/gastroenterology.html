The six films this department runs on

Each earns its place. Cut to size, never inflate — a department publishing four of these steadily beats one publishing all six badly.

  • Endoscopy explained, honestly — "You sleep, we look, it takes seven minutes." The video that converts the most-postponed procedure in medicine.
  • Colonoscopy prep without the dread — The prep is why people cancel. A doctor walking through the day-before, step by step, cuts no-shows measurably.
  • Liver health: the silent organ — Fatty-liver numbers rising in every city. A hepatologist on what the liver forgives and what it doesn't — no blame language.
  • GERD and "acidity" — The reflux patients treat with soda for a decade before the Barrett's diagnosis. The most relatable gastro topic a hospital can film.
  • IBS and the gut-brain talk — Diet, stress and symptoms in one calm film — it answers months of OPD repetition and shares widely among exactly the patients you want.
  • Endoscopy suite tour — The recliner, the monitor, the recovery corner, the friendly staff — filmed so the room stops being imagined as an operating theatre.

Consent and dignity in Gastroenterology video

Gastroenterology stories are embarrassment-sensitive: offer the no-face option as standard, let patients choose first names only, and never stage a procedure reaction. Endoscopy screen output may appear — unidentifiable, no patient data on the monitor's edge. The story is comfort and speed, not the images.

Start with the paperwork: our patient video consent kit is free and printable — signed before any recording, filed with the project record.

Measuring it honestly

The cleanest operational metric in the specialty: scope no-show and preparation-failure rates before and after the prep film. Track day-care bookings against the suite tour's publication, and watch which language version of the endoscopy explainer gets watched to the end — embarrassment topics convert hardest in the viewer's own language.
