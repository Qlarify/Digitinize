Patient Video Consent Form

To be signed before any recording. Give the patient time to read it, and answer questions before signing, not after.

What we are asking

We would like to record a video of you (or your child, named above) for the hospital's public communication. We are asking your permission before anything is recorded.

Please tick what you agree to

Anything you do not tick will not be used. We can also film you so that your face is not shown — please tell the coordinator and they will adjust the framing.

Your medical care will never depend on this

  1. Saying no will not affect your treatment or your relationship with your doctors in any way.
  2. You may change your mind at any time, even after the video is published. Tell the marketing desk and we will take it down within one working day.
  3. The recording belongs to the hospital. No money is paid for appearing in it, and the video may not be used to promote any product or medicine.
  4. Your medical records are not part of this video. Doctors and staff will appear with you only with their own consent.

For patients under 18

The parent or legal guardian signs below. The child's own willingness is also asked for, in words the child understands, before any recording.

Signature of patient / parent / guardian  ·  name in full
Signature of hospital representative (name & designation)
Signature of witness, if the hospital requires one

For the marketing team: file the signed original with the project record. Re-confirm consent by phone if publication happens more than 60 days after signing. If the patient withdraws, log the date, the person informed and the takedown date. Have your legal team review this template once before first use — hospital policies differ.
