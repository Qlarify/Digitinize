Found first: how hospital videos earn search

Patients do not search for your channel; they search their symptom in their own words. This guide covers the five surfaces where a hospital video wins or loses search — title, description, thumbnail, chapters, and retention.

Titles: the patient's words, or nothing

  • Write the title as the question they google. "When does knee pain need a replacement?" — not "Advanced Arthroplasty Services". The first gets searched a thousand times a day; the second gets searched never.
  • City name for OPD-driven topics. "Best cardiology hospital in Bengaluru" is a competitive query; your video competes in it whether you optimise or not.
  • Under 60 characters where possible. Truncation kills the promise the title makes.
  • Never bait. A title the video does not keep trains the algorithm that your channel disappoints — the exact opposite of what SEO needs.

Descriptions and chapters

  • The first 100 characters are the search result. Answer the title's question in them. "Welcome to our channel" in that slot is SEO malpractice.
  • Chapters in plain language: "1:23 Who needs the test" — patients skip to the minute they need, and skipping-to is retention, which is ranking.
  • Phone number and booking link above the fold of the description, plus the languages the video is subtitled in.
  • Tags matter little; the description matters much. Spend the minutes where the algorithm actually reads.

Thumbnails and the first ten seconds

  • A face and three words. A doctor's calm expression outperforms every graphic-design effort a hospital buys.
  • Contrast, not clutter. One subject, readable at 120 pixels wide — because that is the size a phone shows.
  • The first ten seconds answer or promise. Retention at 0:30 is the strongest signal the algorithm uses on a new hospital channel. Long intros are a tax on every future video.

The compounding habits

  • One video, many surfaces: the reel, the shorts cut, the website page, the WhatsApp forward. Each surface feeds the search authority of the others.
  • Playlists by specialty, because autoplay is the cheapest watch-time a hospital earns.
  • Comments answered within two working days. An active comment section is a trust surface and a keyword surface at once.
  • Read the retention graph of every upload. The drop-off minute is next month's fix, for free.
  • Refresh the winners. A two-year-old strong video with a new thumbnail and a sharpened first line often doubles its search traffic. SEO is not only making new things.
