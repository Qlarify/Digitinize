Resources for hospital marketing teams

A year of hospital video, planned on one page

Health days give a hospital's video calendar its rhythm — but the weeks between them decide whether the channel lives. This calendar gives you both: a month-by-month hook for every major health day, and the recurring formats that fill the gaps.

The rules that keep a calendar alive

  • Book the doctors before the days, not for the days. A health-day shoot booked two weeks out gets a calm consultant; one booked two days out gets an apology.
  • Two specialties per month, not ten. The calendar above names one or two departments per month on purpose — a department that knows its month is coming prepares for it.
  • Bank footage in the fat weeks. Record extra evergreen bytes in quiet months; December always needs more than it has.
  • Re-cut, don't re-record. A strong explainer from March can return in September with a new hook line. The calendar is allowed to repeat its winners.
  • Print it and pin it. A calendar on paper in the marketing room survives; one in a slide deck does not.
