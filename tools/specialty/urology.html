Sample shot list for one shoot day

A half-day urology session with one consultant typically yields three to four finished videos.

  1. Urologist, consultation room: stone prevention with a kidney model
  2. Procedure explainer: laser lithotripsy graphics, doctor narrating, no live footage
  3. Men's health consultant: direct, dignified, no euphemism register
  4. Urinary-infection explainer: doctor with simple diagram, when-to-worry rules
  5. Patient story if consented: stone patient back at work in two days
  6. Suite tour: uroflow room, ultrasound couch, friendly nurse explaining
  7. B-roll: water glass poured, salt shaker, kidney model close-ups
  8. Reception: booking the screening, phone line on screen

Questions urology teams ask us

Men's health topics — how direct can we be?

Direct, dignified, clinical. The videos that name the problem plainly outperform euphemism everywhere; the consultant's tone sets the register, and your medical team approves every script.

Do screening videos work for prostate?

They start the family conversation — sons watch them and bring fathers in. The PSA-age explainer is a perennial OPD driver for urology departments.

Can we show procedures?

Stone surgery films best as narrated graphics, not graphic footage. The reassurance is the message: no cut, quick recovery.

How do we reach the repeat stone patients?

Prevention reels get saved and rewatched — the patient who follows them is the one who books the follow-up scan. Publish one and keep it pinned.

Languages?

English plus Kannada and Hindi minimum. Men's-health content converts hardest in the viewer's own language — we recommend widening subtitles for this specialty.

Turnaround?

First cut in two working days, up to three revision rounds.
