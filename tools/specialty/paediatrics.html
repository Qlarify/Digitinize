Sample shot list for one shoot day

A half-day paediatrics session with one consultant typically yields three to four finished videos.

  1. Paediatrician, warm room, not in whites: fever rules, gentle authority
  2. Vaccination room: nurse preparing a shot, schedule chart on screen
  3. Newborn demo with a doll: bathing, holding, swaddling close-ups
  4. NICU corridor and exterior shots: incubators at respectful distance, monitors b-roll
  5. Developmental play: toddler stacking blocks with a therapist
  6. Children's OPD: friendly reception, height chart, waiting play area
  7. Parent testimonial if consented: the midnight fever that ended well
  8. B-roll: tiny hand grips, stethoscope on a teddy bear, colourfully painted walls

Questions paediatrics teams ask us

How do you film around children ethically?

Faces only with a parent's signed consent; many shots use hands, backs and toys. The NICU is filmed to the unit head's rules, and we never stage distress.

Do parents actually choose hospitals from video?

They shortlist from referrals, then check the hospital online — and the paediatrics page is where they linger. Video keeps their attention longest of anything on it.

Is a NICU tour wise? It can look frightening?

Filmed properly — calm staff, warmth, competence — it reassures exactly the families who need the unit, and it is the page's most forwarded film after the fever reel.

Can our senior paediatricians spare the time?

A fixed monthly half-hour yields three to four videos. The fever and vaccination topics do half your paediatrics search traffic alone.

Languages?

English plus Kannada and Hindi minimum; for paediatrics we often add Tamil and Telugu. Parents share videos in their own language most.

Turnaround?

First cut in two working days, up to three revision rounds. Vaccination-drive items get same-week delivery.
